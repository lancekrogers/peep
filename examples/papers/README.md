# Papers

A real-world PDF for trying peep's PDF reader on dense, typeset pages.

```sh
peep examples/papers/bitcoin.pdf
```

Enter opens the paper for reading. Scroll with `j`/`k`, PgUp/PgDn or the mouse
wheel; scrolling past the bottom of a page continues on the next one. `→`/`←`
turn pages, `5G` jumps to page 5, and `R` switches between the page picture and
its text, where `/` searches the current page.

## bitcoin.pdf

Satoshi Nakamoto, *Bitcoin: A Peer-to-Peer Electronic Cash System*, 2008.
Nine pages, unmodified, as published at <https://bitcoin.org/bitcoin.pdf>.

SHA-256: `b1674191a88ec5cdd733e4240a81803105dc412d6c6708d53ab94fc248f4f553`

The paper carries no license statement of its own. bitcoin.org distributes it as
part of the Bitcoin project, which was released under the MIT license. It is not
peep demo content and is not covered by peep's license.
